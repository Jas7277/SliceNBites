About me

Hey everyone, my name is Taylor Clarke and I'm 23 years old. I've been baking ever since I was little up until now. It's always been a big interest of mine. I especially love seeing the reactions of my delicious treats and getting feedback from people! I started my at home business on March 21, 2023 in upper island cove and relocated to Brigus July 18th, 2025! Currently, I offer cheesecakes as my top selling item followed by cookies, brownies, muffins and tartlets! When I was 16, I had a medical mystery happen to me and I still don't have all the answers yet but I was misdiagnosed with celiac disease and fully diagnosed with lactose intolerance. In that journey, I noticed there isn't a lot of fresh baked goods available with both gluten free and lactose free options; combined with family members that have diabetes, this is what inspired me to create the best recipes with these options available so everyone can enjoy a treat! So now I offer gluten free, lactose free, and diabetic friendly options.
*NOTE*: While I am extremely cautious with cross contamination, I cannot guarantee a 100% chance of no cross contamination.
Why should you choose me as your local bakery? I put so much love, care, and joy into all of my desserts, ensuring they're made to perfection and taste amazing! I'm very flexible with my schedule so I can fit you in if need be and I can help you customize your baked goods to however you'd like. I'm even happy to make you items that aren't on the menu! My goal one day is to run my business out of a cozy but old fashioned style bakery built right here in Brigus! I'd like to have a place inside and outside where people can come and sit down and enjoy their homemade treats, catch up with a friend, have a date, or study up until closing time!
