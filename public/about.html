My story

Baking has always been my thing

I've been baking since I was little and never stopped. I'm 23 now, and the part I love most hasn't changed: watching someone take the first bite and seeing their reaction. The feedback, the surprise, the "wait, this is gluten free?", that's the whole reason I do this.

"I want everyone to be able to enjoy a treat, no matter what they can or can't eat."

Why allergen-friendly

Treats made for every diet

When I was 16, a medical mystery turned my life upside down. I was misdiagnosed with celiac disease and later diagnosed with lactose intolerance; and with diabetes in my family, I kept noticing how hard it is to find fresh-baked goods that work for all of us. So I learned to make them myself, without giving up on taste.

Gluten free

Recipes built to be gluten free from the start; not an afterthought.

Lactose free

All the richness you expect, without the lactose your body doesn't want.

Diabetic friendly

Options that let people managing diabetes enjoy dessert again.
